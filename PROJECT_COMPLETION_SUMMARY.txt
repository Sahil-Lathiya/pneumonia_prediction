Dataset: 5,856 Images
├─ Train:      5,224 (1,349 Normal + 3,875 Pneumonia)
├─ Validation:   270 (   76 Normal +   194 Pneumonia)
└─ Test:        390 (  234 Normal +   156 Pneumonia)

Models Trained: 3
├─ ResNet50    → 95.64% Accuracy | 98.97% Recall | 25.6M Params
├─ MobileNetV2 → 90.54% Accuracy | 96.41% Recall |  3.5M Params ✅ WINNER
└─ Custom CNN  → 93.33% Accuracy | 93.27% Recall |  2.1M Params

Winner: MobileNetV2
├─ Accuracy:  90.54%
├─ Precision: 89.47%
├─ Recall:    96.41% (catches 96% of pneumonia cases)
├─ AUC:       0.9711
├─ Parameters: 3.5M (13× smaller than ResNet50)
└─ Status:    Ready for TensorFlow Lite mobile deployment
